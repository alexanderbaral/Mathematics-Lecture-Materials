\chapter*{How to Use These Notes}

These lecture notes are available both as a complete edition and as a
fill-in-the-blank edition. Both editions have the same structure and cover the
same mathematical material. In the fill-in-the-blank edition, selected results
are replaced by writing lines or grids for working through longer calculations.

The notes accompany the lectures. Derivations, examples, and supplementary
comments can be added directly in the spaces provided. Exercises and their
discussion form a separate component of the course.

These notes are prepared for courses at \Institution. Feedback on typographical
errors or mathematical ambiguities is always welcome.
